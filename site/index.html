The score is the model's fall probability for the last second. The alert needs two consecutive scores above 50 and stays on for 4 seconds.

Research prototype

Not a safety system. Trained on a small single-person dataset; measured out-of-video: accuracy 82 %, recall 67 % (about 1 fall in 3 is missed) and false alarms happen. Read the evaluation.

How it works
01PoseMediaPipe finds 33 body landmarks in every frame.
02ResampleThe last second becomes 30 frames, whatever your device speed.
03ClassifyA 1D CNN, exported to ONNX, scores it every ~150 ms.
04AlertTwo consecutive scores above 50 raise the alarm.
Runs 100 % client-side: no video leaves your device. Source, Docker image and API on GitHub · MIT licence
