MediaPipe pose + 1D CNN, running entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or recorded.

Research prototype, not a safety system. Measured out-of-video: accuracy 82 %, recall 67 % (about 1 fall in 3 is missed). See the evaluation.

Stay fully in frame, at 2 to 4 m, in good light. The bar is the fall score of the last second; the alert needs two consecutive scores above 0.5.
How it works
  1. Each camera frame goes through MediaPipe Pose Landmarker (33 body landmarks).
  2. The last second is resampled to 30 frames per second, so the model sees the same time scale as in training whatever your device speed.
  3. A 1D CNN (31 k parameters, ONNX Runtime Web) scores that window every ~150 ms.
  4. Two consecutive scores above 0.5 raise the alert.
