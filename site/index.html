The score is the model's fall probability for the last second. The alert needs two consecutive scores above 50 and stays for 4 seconds.

Research prototype, not a safety system. Trained on a small single-person dataset; measured out-of-video: accuracy 82 %, recall 67 % (about 1 fall in 3 is missed) and false alarms happen. See the evaluation.

PoseMediaPipe finds 33 body landmarks in every frame.
2
ResampleThe last second becomes 30 frames, whatever your device speed.

4
AlertTwo consecutive scores above 50 raise the alarm.
Runs 100 % client-side: no video leaves your device. Source, Docker image and API on GitHub
