Fall detection

MediaPipe pose + 1D CNN, running entirely in your browser. Your video is never uploaded.

Research prototype, not a safety system. Measured out-of-video: accuracy 82 %, recall 67 % (about 1 fall in 3 is missed). Trained on a small single-person dataset. See the evaluation.

How it works
  1. Frames are sampled at 30 per second (at most 150).
  2. MediaPipe Pose Landmarker finds 33 body landmarks in each frame.
  3. A 1D CNN (31 k parameters, ONNX) scores each 30-frame window (stride 15).
  4. The clip is flagged if any window scores above 0.5.
Source, Docker image and API: github.com/gabrielkpo/Fall-detection
